Your times tables hide lots of patterns. In this lesson you will find factor pairs, and learn the square numbers and cube numbers by heart.

## Factors and factor pairs

When you multiply two numbers, the answer is the **product**. In $3 \times 4 = 12$, the product is 12.

A **factor** of a number divides into it exactly, with nothing left over. Two factors that multiply to make the number are a **factor pair**.

### Example: the factors of 24

Work through in order, starting at 1:

- $1 \times 24$, $2 \times 12$, $3 \times 8$, $4 \times 6$
- 5 doesn't go into 24. The next number, 6, is already in a pair, so you can stop.

The factors of 24 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 24.

> **Watch out:** A **multiple** is a number in the times table. 6 is a *factor* of 24, but 48 is a *multiple* of 24.

Square numbers make square patterns of dots. Learn these by heart:

| $1^2$ | $2^2$ | $3^2$ | $4^2$ | $5^2$ | $6^2$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 | 9 | 16 | 25 | 36 |

| $7^2$ | $8^2$ | $9^2$ | $10^2$ | $11^2$ | $12^2$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 49 | 64 | 81 | 100 | 121 | 144 |

> **Watch out:** $5^2$ means $5 \times 5 = 25$. It does **not** mean $5 \times 2$.

A cube number tells you how many small cubes make a bigger solid cube. Learn the first five by heart:

$1^3 = 1$, $2^3 = 8$, $3^3 = 27$, $4^3 = 64$, $5^3 = 125$

### Example

$4^3 = 4 \times 4 \times 4$. Work out $4 \times 4 = 16$ first, then $16 \times 4 = 64$.

> **Tip:** 64 is special. It is a square number ($8^2$) **and** a cube number ($4^3$).

## Putting it together

### Example: tiles

Ella has 50 square tiles. She wants to make the biggest square she can. The biggest square number that isn't more than 50 is 49, because $7 \times 7 = 49$. Ella makes a 7 by 7 square and has $50 - 49 = 1$ tile left over.

### Example: which is bigger?

Is $3^3$ or $5^2$ bigger? $3^3 = 27$ and $5^2 = 25$, so $3^3$ is bigger.

**Quick tips**

- Find factor pairs in order, starting with 1. Stop when the pairs start to repeat.
- A square number has a factor pair with the same number twice, like $6 \times 6 = 36$.
- Squared means two of the number multiplied together. Cubed means three.
