Probability is the maths of chance. It tells you how likely something is to happen, from a raffle draw to the weather. In this lesson you'll work out probabilities by thinking carefully about all the things that could happen.

## Outcomes, events and the probability scale

An **outcome** is one possible result. When you roll a die, the outcomes are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6. The list of all possible outcomes is the **sample space**.

An **event** is one outcome, or a group of outcomes. "Rolling an even number" is an event made of the outcomes 2, 4 and 6.

The **probability** of an event is a number from 0 to 1.

- Rolling a 7 on a normal die is **impossible**: probability 0.
- Getting heads when you toss a coin has an **even chance**: probability $\frac{1}{2}$.
- The sun rising tomorrow is **certain**: probability 1.

## Calculating probability

When all the outcomes are **equally likely**:

$$P(\text{event}) = \frac{\text{number of ways the event can happen}}{\text{total number of possible outcomes}}$$

This spinner has 6 equal sections.

## Fractions, decimals and percentages

A probability can be written as a fraction, a decimal from 0 to 1, or a percentage from 0% to 100%.

| Fraction | Decimal | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| $\frac{1}{2}$ | 0.5 | 50% |
| $\frac{1}{4}$ | 0.25 | 25% |
| $\frac{3}{4}$ | 0.75 | 75% |
| $\frac{1}{5}$ | 0.2 | 20% |
| $\frac{1}{10}$ | 0.1 | 10% |

To change a fraction to a percentage, make an equivalent fraction out of 100: $\frac{7}{20} = \frac{35}{100} = 0.35 = 35\%$.

### Example

Which is most likely: $\frac{3}{5}$, 0.55 or 58%?

Write them all as percentages: $\frac{3}{5} = \frac{60}{100} = 60\%$, $0.55 = 55\%$, and 58%. The biggest is 60%, so $\frac{3}{5}$ is the most likely.

## Complements

The **complement** of an event is everything in the sample space that is **not** the event. The complement of "rolling a 6" is "not rolling a 6": 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5.

An event and its complement always add to 1 (or 100%):

$$P(\text{not } A) = 1 - P(A)$$

### Examples

- $P(\text{rolling a } 6) = \frac{1}{6}$, so $P(\text{not rolling a } 6) = 1 - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$.
- The chance of rain is 0.3, so the chance of no rain is $1 - 0.3 = 0.7$.
- A ferry is on time 85% of the time, so it is not on time $100\% - 85\% = 15\%$ of the time.

### Example

$P(\text{one head and one tail})$: HT and TH both fit, so it is $\frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$.

> **Watch out:** "One of each" can happen in **two** ways (HT and TH). That's why there are four outcomes, not three.

For two dice, a **two-way table** shows all $6 \times 6 = 36$ outcomes. A total of 7 appears 6 times (1 + 6, 2 + 5, 3 + 4, 4 + 3, 5 + 2, 6 + 1), so $P(\text{total of } 7) = \frac{6}{36} = \frac{1}{6}$.
