Temperatures below zero, money that is owed and depths under the sea all use negative numbers. In this lesson you will place integers on a number line, put them in order, and add and subtract them.

## What is an integer?

**Integers** are the whole numbers, their negatives, and zero: …, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, …

**Positive** numbers are to the right of 0 on a **number line**. **Negative** numbers are to the left. We write them with a negative sign: −4 is "negative four". **Zero** is neither positive nor negative.

The number line carries on forever in both directions, so there is no biggest or smallest integer.

Integers are never fractions or decimals. 3.5 and $\frac{1}{2}$ are not integers.

## Ordering integers

On a number line, numbers get bigger as you go right and smaller as you go left.

- Every negative number is less than 0, and less than every positive number.
- With two negative numbers, the one further from 0 is smaller. −9 is less than −2, because −9 is further to the left.

Think of temperature: −9 °C is colder than −2 °C.

We use < for "is less than" and > for "is greater than":

$-9 < -2$ and $3 > -5$

The additive inverse of 5 is −5, and the additive inverse of −5 is 5:

$-5 + 5 = 0$

It works like money. If you owe \$5 and then you are given \$5, you have \$0.

### Example

What is the additive inverse of −23? It is 23, because $-23 + 23 = 0$.

Additive inverses give you shortcuts. In $-8 + 6 + 8$, the −8 and the 8 make 0, so the answer is 6.

### Example 2

$2 - 6$: start at 2 and move 6 to the left. It takes 2 steps to reach 0, then 4 more. The answer is **−4**.

**Adding a negative** number moves you left, just like subtracting: $5 + (-8) = 5 - 8 = -3$.

**Subtracting a negative** is the same as adding its additive inverse: $4 - (-3) = 4 + 3 = 7$. Taking away a debt leaves you better off.

## Integers in real life

- **Temperature:** a rise means add, and a fall means subtract. If it is −4 °C and it warms up by 9 °C, it becomes $-4 + 9 = 5$ °C.
- **Money:** money you owe is negative. If you have \$10 and spend \$15, your balance is $10 - 15 = -5$. You owe \$5.
- **Height:** sea level is 0. A diver 12 m below sea level is at −12 m.

### Example

One day in Tekapo the highest temperature was 6 °C and the lowest was −5 °C. What is the difference? From −5 up to 0 is 5 degrees, and from 0 up to 6 is 6 more. The difference is $5 + 6 = 11$ °C.

> **Tip:** When two numbers are on opposite sides of 0, add their distances from 0 to find how far apart they are.
