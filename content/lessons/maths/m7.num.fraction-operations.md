How much is $\frac{3}{4}$ of the class? How many $\frac{1}{4}$ m pieces can you cut from 3 m of ribbon? In this lesson you will find fractions of amounts, multiply and divide fractions by whole numbers, and work back to the whole.

## Finding a fraction of an amount

A **unit fraction** has a numerator of 1, like $\frac{1}{5}$. To find $\frac{1}{5}$ of a number, split it into 5 equal parts: divide by 5.

To find a fraction like $\frac{3}{5}$, first find $\frac{1}{5}$, then take 3 of those parts.

### Example
$\frac{3}{4}$ of 32: $\frac{1}{4}$ of 32 is $32 \div 4 = 8$, so $\frac{3}{4}$ of 32 is $3 \times 8 = 24$.

This works for an **improper fraction** too. $\frac{5}{4}$ of 32 is $5 \times 8 = 40$. That is more than 32, because $\frac{5}{4}$ is more than one **whole**.

> **Tip:** Divide by the denominator, then multiply by the numerator.

## Multiplying a whole number by a fraction

Multiply the numerator by the whole number. The denominator stays the same, because the pieces are still fifths.

$3 \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3 \times 2}{5} = \frac{6}{5} = 1\frac{1}{5}$

### Example
$6 \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{18}{4}$. Simplify: $\frac{18}{4} = \frac{9}{2} = 4\frac{1}{2}$. Always give the answer in **simplest form**.

> **Watch out:** Don't multiply the denominator by the whole number as well. $3 \times \frac{2}{5}$ is not $\frac{6}{15}$.

When you divide a fraction by a whole number, each piece is cut into smaller pieces. So multiply the denominator by the whole number:

$\frac{2}{3} \div 4 = \frac{2}{3 \times 4} = \frac{2}{12} = \frac{1}{6}$

If the numerator divides exactly, you can divide it instead: $\frac{6}{7} \div 3 = \frac{2}{7}$ (6 sevenths shared into 3 groups is 2 sevenths each).

$3 \div \frac{1}{4} = 12$

Dividing by a unit fraction is the same as multiplying by its denominator. The answer is bigger than the number you started with, because the pieces are smaller than 1.

### Example
Isla has 5 m of ribbon. She cuts it into $\frac{1}{3}$ m pieces. $5 \div \frac{1}{3} = 5 \times 3 = 15$ pieces.

3 parts are worth 24, so 1 part is $24 \div 3 = 8$. The whole is 5 parts: $5 \times 8 = 40$.

Check: $\frac{3}{5}$ of 40 is $40 \div 5 \times 3 = 24$ ✓

**Putting it together:**
- "Of" means multiply: divide by the denominator, multiply by the numerator.
- Fraction ÷ whole number: the pieces get smaller, so the denominator gets bigger.
- Whole number ÷ unit fraction: count how many pieces fit, so the answer gets bigger.
- To find the whole, find one part first.
