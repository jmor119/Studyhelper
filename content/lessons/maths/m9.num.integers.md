An **integer** is a whole number that can be positive, negative or zero: …, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, …. **Negative** numbers show up in temperatures, bank balances and depths below sea level, so you need to calculate with them confidently and in the right order.

## Adding and subtracting integers

Picture a number line. Adding moves you **right**; subtracting moves you **left**.

- $-3 + 8 = 5$: start at −3 and move 8 to the right.
- $4 - 9 = -5$: start at 4 and move 9 to the left, past zero.

When the second number is negative, rewrite the calculation first:

| You see | It means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| $+\,(-)$ | subtract | $6 + (-2) = 6 - 2 = 4$ |
| $-\,(-)$ | add | $6 - (-2) = 6 + 2 = 8$ |

Subtracting a negative is like taking away a debt: you end up better off.

### Example

$-2 - (-6) = -2 + 6 = 4$

> **Watch out:** $-7 - 3$ is $-10$, not $-4$. You are moving further left from −7.

## Multiplying and dividing integers

Multiply or divide the numbers as usual, then decide the sign:

| Signs | Answer | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| same | positive | $(-6) \times (-4) = 24$, $\;(-20) \div (-5) = 4$ |
| different | negative | $(-6) \times 4 = -24$, $\;20 \div (-5) = -4$ |

With more than two numbers, count the negatives. An **even** number of negative factors gives a positive answer; an **odd** number gives a negative answer.

### Example

$(-2) \times (-3) \times (-5)$: three negatives (odd), and $2 \times 3 \times 5 = 30$, so the answer is $-30$.

> **Tip:** The sign rules for ÷ are exactly the same as for ×.

## The order of operations (GEMA)

The **order of operations** is the agreed order for doing a calculation, so everyone gets the same answer. Remember it as **GEMA**:

1. **G**rouping: brackets first (a fraction bar groups the top and the bottom too).
2. **E**xponents: powers such as $3^2$.
3. **M**ultiplication and division, working **left to right**.
4. **A**ddition and subtraction, working **left to right**.

### Example

$18 - 3 \times (2 - 6)$

1. Bracket: $2 - 6 = -4$
2. Multiply: $3 \times (-4) = -12$
3. Subtract: $18 - (-12) = 18 + 12 = 30$

> **Watch out:** × and ÷ have equal priority, so go left to right: $20 \div 4 \times 5 = 5 \times 5 = 25$, not $20 \div 20 = 1$. The same goes for + and −: $10 - 4 + 3 = 9$.

## Powers of negative numbers

Brackets make a big difference with powers.

- $(-3)^2 = (-3) \times (-3) = 9$. The bracket means the whole of −3 is squared.
- $-3^2 = -(3 \times 3) = -9$. Exponents come before the minus sign, so only the 3 is squared.

So $(-3)^2$ is **not** the same as $-3^2$.

The same idea works for other powers. $(-2)^3 = (-2) \times (-2) \times (-2) = -8$, because three negatives give a negative. $(-2)^4 = 16$, because four negatives give a positive.

> **Tip:** Many calculators treat $-3^2$ as $-(3^2) = -9$. If you mean "negative three, squared", type the brackets.

### Example

$(-4)^2 - 4^2 = 16 - 16 = 0$, but $(-4)^2 - (-4^2) = 16 - (-16) = 32$.

## Reciprocals

Two numbers are **reciprocals** of each other if they multiply to give 1.

- The reciprocal of $\frac{2}{3}$ is $\frac{3}{2}$, because $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{6}{6} = 1$.
- The reciprocal of 5 is $\frac{1}{5}$, because $5 \times \frac{1}{5} = 1$.
- The reciprocal of $-4$ is $-\frac{1}{4}$. It stays negative, because negative × negative = positive.

To find a reciprocal, write the number as a fraction and **flip it** (swap the numerator and the denominator). For a mixed number, change it to an improper fraction first: $2\frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}$, so its reciprocal is $\frac{2}{5}$.

Zero has **no** reciprocal: nothing multiplied by 0 gives 1.

Reciprocals matter because dividing by a number is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal. You'll use this a lot with fractions.

## Exam tips

- **Rewrite double signs first.** Change $+(-)$ to $-$ and $-(-)$ to $+$ before you calculate.
- **Write each GEMA step on a new line.** Most marks are lost by doing two steps at once in your head.
- **Look for brackets around negatives** in powers: $(-a)^2$ is positive, but $-a^2$ is negative.
- **Check the sign at the end.** Count the negative factors: even means positive, odd means negative.
- **Reciprocal questions:** check your answer by multiplying. You should get exactly 1.
