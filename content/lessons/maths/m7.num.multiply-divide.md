Multiplying and dividing big numbers without a calculator is all about splitting numbers into parts you can handle. In this lesson you will multiply using place value, divide by one- and two-digit numbers, decide what to do with a remainder, and use estimates to check your answers.

## Multiplying with place value

The answer to a multiplication is called the **product**.

To multiply bigger numbers, split one number into hundreds, tens and ones. Multiply each part, then add the answers.

### Example

$47 \times 23$. Split 23 into $20 + 3$.

- $47 \times 20 = 940$ (work out $47 \times 2 = 94$, then $\times 10$)
- $47 \times 3 = 141$

$940 + 141 = 1081$

## Dividing

In $852 \div 6 = 142$:

- 852 is the **dividend**: the number being divided.
- 6 is the **divisor**: the number you divide by.
- 142 is the **quotient**: the answer.

A good way to divide is to split the dividend into chunks that the divisor goes into easily.

### Example

$852 \div 6$: split 852 into $600 + 240 + 12$.

$600 \div 6 = 100$, $240 \div 6 = 40$ and $12 \div 6 = 2$. So $852 \div 6 = 142$.

Check by multiplying: $142 \times 6 = 852$ ✓

**Two-digit divisors.** Write a few easy multiples first. For $864 \div 24$: $24 \times 10 = 240$, $24 \times 20 = 480$, $24 \times 30 = 720$. Then $864 - 720 = 144$, and $24 \times 6 = 144$. So $864 \div 24 = 30 + 6 = 36$.

## Remainders

Sometimes the divisor doesn't go in exactly. The amount left over is the **remainder**.

$327 \div 5$: $5 \times 65 = 325$, and $327 - 325 = 2$. So $327 \div 5 = 65$ remainder 2.

A remainder can be written three ways.

| Whole number remainder | Fraction | Decimal |
|---|---|---|
| 65 r 2 | $65\frac{2}{5}$ | 65.4 |

For the fraction, the 2 left over is shared between 5, which makes $\frac{2}{5}$. As a decimal, $\frac{2}{5} = \frac{4}{10} = 0.4$.

## What should you do with the remainder?

The question tells you what to do with the remainder.

- **Round up** when everyone or everything must fit. 75 students, and each bus holds 20: $75 \div 20 = 3$ r 15, so you need **4** buses.
- **Round down** when only full groups count. 75 eggs packed in cartons of 12 make **6** full cartons.
- **Give the remainder** when the question asks what is left over. There are **3** eggs left over.
- **Use a decimal** for money. \$75 shared between 4 people is **\$18.75** each.
- **Use a fraction** for things you can cut. 7 pizzas shared between 4 people is $1\frac{3}{4}$ pizzas each.

## Estimate to check

An **estimate** is a quick, rough answer. Round each number, then work it out in your head.

- $47 \times 23$ is about $50 \times 20 = 1000$. The exact answer, 1081, is close, so it is a sensible size. If you got 10 810 or 108, you would know something went wrong.
- For division, choose numbers that divide easily: $2389 \div 6$ is about $2400 \div 6 = 400$.

> **Tip:** Estimate before you start and check after you finish. An estimate catches place-value slips, like a missing zero.

> **Tip:** You can check a division by multiplying. $65 \times 5 + 2 = 327$, so $327 \div 5 = 65$ r 2 is right.
