Formulas are used everywhere: to work out a phone bill, convert temperatures or find the area of a section of land. Using one means swapping letters for numbers. Sometimes you also need to rearrange a formula to find a different quantity.

## Substituting into expressions

To **substitute** means to replace each letter with its value, then work out the answer.

- Write the multiplication signs back in: $3a$ becomes $3 \times a$.
- Put negative numbers in brackets so you don't lose the sign.
- Follow the order of operations (GEMA): grouping, exponents, then $\times$ and $\div$, then $+$ and $-$.

### Example

Find $3a - 2b$ when $a = 4$ and $b = -5$.

$3a - 2b = 3 \times 4 - 2 \times (-5) = 12 - (-10) = 12 + 10 = 22$

### Example

Find $\dfrac{p + q}{4}$ when $p = 13$ and $q = -1$.

The fraction line acts like a bracket, so do the top first: $\dfrac{13 + (-1)}{4} = \dfrac{12}{4} = 3$.

## Powers and negative numbers

Squaring a negative number gives a positive answer: $(-3)^2 = (-3) \times (-3) = 9$.

Be careful about what is being squared:

| Expression | When $x = -3$ | Why |
|---|---|---|
| $x^2$ | $(-3)^2 = 9$ | the whole $-3$ is squared |
| $2x^2$ | $2 \times 9 = 18$ | square first, then multiply |
| $(2x)^2$ | $(-6)^2 = 36$ | the bracket is done first |
| $-x^2$ | $-(9) = -9$ | square first, then apply the minus |

> **Watch out:** $2x^2$ means $2 \times x^2$. Only the $x$ is squared, not the 2.

> **Tip:** On a calculator, always type negative numbers in brackets: `(-3)²`, not `-3²`.

## Using formulas

A **formula** is a rule connecting two or more quantities, such as $P = 2l + 2w$ for the perimeter of a rectangle. The letter on its own on the left, $P$, is the **subject** of the formula.

### Example

A plumber's charge in dollars is $C = 65 + 48h$, where $h$ is the number of hours. Find the cost of a 3-hour job.

$C = 65 + 48 \times 3 = 65 + 144 = 209$, so the job costs \$209.

### Working backwards

If you know the subject and want another letter, substitute what you know and solve the equation.

The same plumber charged \$305. How long was the job?

$305 = 65 + 48h \;\Rightarrow\; 240 = 48h \;\Rightarrow\; h = 5$ hours.

> **Tip:** Always include units in your final answer (cm, m/s, \$, hours).

## Changing the subject

To **rearrange** a formula means to change its **subject**: get a different letter on its own. You use the same balancing steps as solving an equation, undoing operations in reverse order.

### Example

Make $w$ the subject of $P = 2l + 2w$.

1. Subtract $2l$ from both sides: $P - 2l = 2w$
2. Divide both sides by 2: $\dfrac{P - 2l}{2} = w$

So $w = \dfrac{P - 2l}{2}$, which can also be written $w = \dfrac{P}{2} - l$.

### Example

Make $t$ the subject of $d = st$ (distance = speed × time).

$t$ is multiplied by $s$, so divide both sides by $s$: $t = \dfrac{d}{s}$.

> **Tip:** Rearranging first is useful when you need to use a formula many times, for example to find the width of several rectangles.

> **Typing answers in this app:** use lowercase letters, and type fractions with `/` and brackets, e.g. `(p - 2l)/2`.

## Exam tips

- **Show the substitution step.** Writing $3 \times (-4)$ before working it out earns method marks and avoids sign errors.
- **Brackets around negatives,** especially when squaring.
- **GEMA:** powers before multiplying, multiplying before adding.
- **Working backwards?** Substitute everything you know, then solve the equation that is left.
- **Rearranging:** whatever you do to one side, do to the *whole* of the other side.
- **Check a rearrangement** by substituting numbers: if $l = 3$ and $w = 2$, then $P = 10$, and $\frac{10 - 2 \times 3}{2} = 2$ ✓
