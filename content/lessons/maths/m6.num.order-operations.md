What is $3 + 4 \times 2$? If you add first you get 14, but if you multiply first you get 11. Mathematicians agreed on one order, so that everyone gets the same answer.

## The order of operations

An **operation** is something you do to numbers: add (+), subtract (−), multiply (×) or divide (÷). An **expression** is a group of numbers and operations, like $3 + 4 \times 2$.

The **order of operations** tells you which part to work out first:

### Example

$3 + 4 \times 2$

× comes before +, so multiply first: $4 \times 2 = 8$.

Then add: $3 + 8 = 11$.

### Example

$12 - 8 \div 2$

÷ comes before −, so divide first: $8 \div 2 = 4$.

Then subtract: $12 - 4 = 8$.

## Brackets and squares

**Brackets** ( ) show which part to do first. They can change the answer:

- $(3 + 4) \times 2 = 7 \times 2 = 14$
- $3 + 4 \times 2 = 3 + 8 = 11$

**Squares** come next, before × and ÷. Remember that $3^2$ means $3 \times 3$, and we say "3 **squared**".

### Examples

- $2 + 3^2 = 2 + 9 = 11$
- $(2 + 3)^2 = 5^2 = 25$
- $3 \times 4^2 = 3 \times 16 = 48$

> **Watch out:** In $3 \times 4^2$, only the 4 is squared. Don't work out $3 \times 4 = 12$ first.

## Working from left to right

× and ÷ are partners: they have the same rank. When an expression has both, work from **left to right**. The same goes for + and −.

### Examples

- $20 - 6 + 4$: the − is on the left, so do it first. $20 - 6 = 14$, then $14 + 4 = 18$.
- $24 \div 4 \times 2$: the ÷ is on the left, so do it first. $24 \div 4 = 6$, then $6 \times 2 = 12$.

A longer one: $5 + 18 \div 3 \times 2 - 4$

1. ÷ and × first, from the left: $18 \div 3 = 6$, then $6 \times 2 = 12$.
2. Now it is $5 + 12 - 4$. From the left: $5 + 12 = 17$, then $17 - 4 = 13$.

> **Watch out:** $20 - 6 + 4$ is not $20 - 10$. Don't add first just because + is in the order before −.

## Brackets in problems

Brackets help you write one calculation for a whole problem.

### Example

Tama buys 3 pies at \$4 each and a drink for \$2. He pays with \$20. How much change does he get?

The cost is $3 \times 4 + 2$. The change is \$20 take away the **whole** cost, so put the cost in brackets:

$20 - (3 \times 4 + 2) = 20 - (12 + 2) = 20 - 14 = 6$

Tama gets \$6 change.

### Example: where do the brackets go?

Make $2 + 3 \times 4 = 20$ true. Without brackets the answer is 14. Put brackets around $2 + 3$: $(2 + 3) \times 4 = 5 \times 4 = 20$ ✓
